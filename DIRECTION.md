# Vagora — art direction (v6 rebuild)

## Visual thesis
The product is a sheet of glass a shopper stands in front of, and the page keeps the v5 discipline around it: a white canvas, ink (`#0F0F0F`) for the product chapter and pure black for the closing chapters as the dramatic surfaces, ink for text and chrome, and construction by whitespace. What v6 adds is pacing — the film owns the first viewport outright, the dock arrives, and each act is choreographed rather than stacked.

## Hero focal asset
The intro film (`vagora-intro`), full viewport, with nothing written over it. The poster is the static first frame; under reduced motion the poster is the hero and the film is one click away. No WebGL.

## Type hierarchy
Two voices. **Fragment Mono** (regular only, one 14px tier, one 0.02em tracking, never bold) is Vagora's micro compositional language: every eyebrow — VAGORA IS, ACT ONE · ON THE FLOOR, THE VAGORA MIRROR, ACT TWO · IN THE BUSINESS, THE CONSOLE, BEFORE YOU COMMIT, REQUEST A DEMO and each feature classification — together with the indexes and sequence numbers, RETAIL IMPACT, the specification and FAQ indexes, and the console's system metadata (ESTATE OVERVIEW, ILLUSTRATIVE, SAMPLE DATA). It is notation and never decoration: no brackets, coordinates, timestamps or terminal styling. It has one colour per surface — 55% ink on light, 52% white on dark — and nothing else: an eyebrow, an index, RETAIL IMPACT, an act label, a specification number and the console's metadata are all set at the same strength, because the voice does not change with what it happens to be labelling. **Switzer** carries everything human and everything interactive — the whole editorial voice, in an extremely small vocabulary of sizes repeated across radically different compositions: display 60→160px (the close alone), lead 42→84px (the manifesto proposition), chapter 36→64px (the two act openings), section 28→48px (the Vagora Mirror, the Console, Before you commit — `.h2` and `.h3` share it), feature ~22px (act feature titles and FAQ questions), body 18→22px (explanation, ledes, FAQ questions at heading weight), small 16→17px (secondary and compact information, specification facts). Headings sit at 500 on light surfaces and 450 on dark (white blooms), body at 400 everywhere: scale creates the authority, weight the refinement. Display runs at 0.9 / −0.045em, statement at 0.94 / −0.035em. Hierarchy comes from scale contrast, position, spacing, image and composition — never from a component-specific size. Outside the system: the dock's own type, the console's metric numerals, the curtain count, the footer wordmark. The identity: large Switzer is fashion, body Switzer is human, small Fragment Mono is precision. An eyebrow always sits the same distance above the heading it names — one site-wide value, corrected per tier so the *seen* gap is equal under a 48px section heading and a 160px close, not merely the same margin. Sections never set their own.

## Colour system
Monochrome, and two surfaces only: `#FFFFFF` and `#000000`. There is no third ground — no near-white, no near-black. Light world: `#FFFFFF` surface, `#0F0F0F` ink (ink only, never a surface), text secondary at 62%, muted at 45%, notation at 55%, rule at 10%. Dark world: `#000000` surface for the hero, the Vagora Mirror, the close and the footer alike, white text, secondary 68%, muted 45%, notation 52%, rule 14%. Every other tone (frost, media ground, control hover, dock wash) is an alpha of ink or white, never a new hex. Status hues (`#0F7A52`, `#8A5A00`, `#C0342E` with their tints) for the console pills only.

## Foundations
Page gutter `clamp(1.5rem, 1rem + 1.1vw, 3rem)` (≈32px at 1440, 38px at 2000, 48px max); art-directed media may run past it to the viewport edge, and nothing wraps the site in a global max-width. Reading widths 42rem / 30rem for copy only. Spacing vocabulary 8·12·16·24·32·48·64·96·128·160 and three section rhythms (sm/md/lg). Media radius 0, control radius 8, pill 999; the device frame keeps its hardware geometry. Motion primitives 180 / 320 / 700ms on `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`; signature choreography (reveal, dock arrival, flip) keeps its authored timings.

## Section sequence
1. **Intro** (black, film) — curtain with the mark → film takes the viewport → dock rises.
2. **Manifesto** (white) — one paragraph, word-by-word opacity scrub.
3. **Act one · On the floor** (white) — the system Act two mirrors. — a large retail world with editorial annotation: a 60.5vw × 71svh media stage flush to the right viewport edge, sharp-cornered and sticky, with the five step annotations in a narrow left column. Two phases: the opening (label, display title, supporting statement on the media's left edge, media entering) clears the viewport before State 01 reaches its reading position; the same media plane carries through. Differential scaling (stage grows fastest, headlines moderately, body barely). The sticky stage exists only from 1100px wide and 600px tall; below that every state is one editorial scene in normal flow — image, index + category, headline, description — with generous air between states and no script. On the wide layout a step activates when the top of its reading block crosses 72% of the viewport (the same band, crossed upward, reactivates the earlier step) and the stage changes in 300ms. Retail impact lines withheld from presentation for now.
4. **The Vagora Mirror** (`#0F0F0F`; not an act — the product connecting Act one and Act two) — a dark sculptural product chapter. Macro: the complete Vagora Mirror standing in its own dark studio as a large product field on the left — height-driven (≈80svh), left of the chapter's centre, its photographic environment merging into the surface through a subtle, purely functional edge mask, nothing drawn around or over it; precise white editorial annotation on the right (label `THE VAGORA MIRROR`, two-line section-tier headline, one paragraph at 36ch), beginning around the product's upper third. Micro: a quiet technical specification rail beneath both — one structural rule, four territories, label-tier indexes and functional-tier facts, four columns → 2 × 2 → stacked. A 1680px local stage keeps the two sides one composition on large monitors; extra width becomes outer dark space. Below 1200px: label, headline, copy, then the product media large and immersive, then the rail. The media is `vagora-mirror-dark.jpeg` for now; the photoshoot video of the real Mirror waking up takes the same container. No scripted wake. The product noun is “the Vagora Mirror” — never unit, kiosk, terminal, station.
5. **Act two · In the business** (white) — the deliberate mirrored second act: Act one's interaction system exactly, from the chapter opening to the last feature. The same media plane, the same sticky behaviour, the same rail rhythm, the same activation band and the same crossfade — but the plane runs to the left viewport edge and the narrative passes it on the right, where Act one reads text-left and world-right. Below the same breakpoint the stage steps aside in both acts and every feature becomes one editorial scene in normal flow: image, index and category, headline, description, retail impact.
6. **The console** (white) — the calm proof chapter: eyebrow, statement and paragraph on the left, one coherent console object on the right. Everything the console knows stays inside that object — sharp, minimal rules, no shadow, no browser chrome — so it reads as a product placed in an editorial page rather than data scattered across it.
7. **Handoff** (white) — the physical reset: the proof photograph full-bleed at ~70vh, strongly cropped, nothing written on it. Editorial information design gives way to the room.
8. **Before you commit** (white) — six answers, all open, numbered.
9. **See it in your store** (black) — display-scale ask, magnetic button, printed + copyable address.
10. **Footer** (black) — the display wordmark full width, colophon. The dock is the only navigation, fixed bottom-centre.

## Chapter annotation
Both acts share one annotation system, and the tension is the point: a very large chapter heading, a very large image, and small precise copy beside it. The index alone in Fragment Mono — (01) — then a compact title at body scale in medium weight, a description at small scale, a short rule, the mono RETAIL IMPACT label and its sentence. Everything ink, everything on one left axis, all the air outside the block. Hierarchy comes from scale, weight, font and structure; grey is not used to fake it.

## Motion narrative
GSAP 3 + ScrollTrigger; Lenis as the sole smooth-scroll engine. Headings split word-by-word with an unsplit visually-hidden accessible name. Sections reveal once; only the manifesto is scrubbed; the floor's mirror state follows the active step and Act two's evidence stage follows the feature being read. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: no Lenis, no curtain, all states rendered immediately, no scrub, film paused on its poster.

## Three.js decision
No. The hero is the film; a shader would be ornament here.
