What happens
on the floor.

Five things your customers can do with Vagora that aren’t possible anywhere else on your floor.

  1. (01)

    The garment, rendered on the customer in live 3D.

    It tracks the customer’s movement in real time, so the garment responds as they move.

    Every size and colourway, on screen.

    Customers can explore the full catalogue, with each option checked against live stock before it is shown.

    Retail impact

    The buying decision is no longer limited to what physically fits on the rail.

    The whole look, before anything is fetched.

    Customers can combine pieces on themselves to see how the complete outfit comes together.

    Retail impact

    More pieces enter consideration without staff having to suggest them one by one.

    Made-to-order, before the first cut.

    Unstitched cloth and made-to-order garments can be rendered on the customer before production begins.

    Retail impact

    Orders are taken against something the customer has already seen, so cloth is cut to demand rather than forecast.

  5. (05)

    The campaign, with the customer in it.

    Vagora places the customer inside campaign scenes built around the collection.

    Retail impact

    The campaign becomes an in-store experience rather than something the customer only sees around them.

FAQ

Before you commit.

  1. 01

    What happens to customer images?

    The camera is active only during a session and nothing is stored. There is no enrolment, no account and no face database. A photo the customer chooses to keep goes to their phone and leaves the mirror when the session ends.

  2. 02

    Will it work with our catalogue?

    It reads the product feed you already publish, and stock syncs live from your system. Nothing appears on the mirror that the store cannot produce.

  3. 03

    What do staff have to do?

    Nothing during a session. The mirror starts, runs and ends on its own. Staff meet the customer at the counter with a list that is already there, which is where a person is worth more than a screen.

  4. 04

    How do we know whether it worked?

    Sessions, try-ons per session, scans to phone and scans to counter, by store and by garment, in the console from the first day. We have no benchmark to compare you against yet, and will not invent one.

  5. 05

    What does a rollout involve?

    One store first, on a fixed term, with the measure agreed in writing before installation. After that, catalogue, content and configuration are managed centrally, so each further store is a delivery date.

  6. 06

    What does it cost?

    We quote per estate. The price depends on the number of mirrors, the stores they go into and what your catalogue and stock systems need on our side. Ask and we will put a number in writing.
